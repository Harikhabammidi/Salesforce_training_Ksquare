=========================================
Validation Rule 1
=========================================

Rule:
Student CGPA must be greater than or equal to the Job's minimum CGPA.

Formula:
Student__r.CGPA__c < Job__r.Minimum_CGPA__c


=========================================
Validation Rule 2
=========================================

Rule:
Application Date cannot be after the Job Closing Date.

Formula:
Application_Date__c > Job__r.Closing_Date__c


=========================================
Validation Rule 3
=========================================

Rule:
Mandatory fields cannot be left blank.

Formula:
OR(
    ISBLANK(Student__c),
    ISBLANK(Job__c),
    ISBLANK(TEXT(Status__c))
)


=========================================
Important Note: Duplicate Applications
=========================================

Rule:
A student should not be allowed to apply for the same Job more than once.

Note:
A Validation Rule cannot check other Application records for duplicates.

For duplicate prevention, use Duplicate Rules and Matching Rules.
For more complex duplicate logic, Flow or Apex can be used.
